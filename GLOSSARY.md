# FjøsFinn

A marketplace where barn gnomes trade things with each other. The site's text is in Norwegian, so the canonical terms are the Norwegian words.

## Language

**Nisse**:
A barn gnome who uses FjøsFinn. Known only by the name and the Gård they type in; there are no accounts.
_Avoid_: User, bruker, konto, seller

**Gård**:
The farm a Nisse lives on, written as free text. It is not a separate thing that can be chosen from a list.
_Avoid_: Location, adresse

**Annonse**:
An offer from one Nisse to trade away one thing, with a title, a description and what the Nisse wants back (Byttes mot).
_Avoid_: Listing, post, ad, produkt

**Byttes mot**:
What the Nisse wants in exchange for the thing in the Annonse, written as free text. FjøsFinn has no money or prices.
_Avoid_: Pris, price, payment

**Bytte**:
The trade itself, agreed between two Nisser outside FjøsFinn.
_Avoid_: Kjøp, salg, transaction
